Betula: mark and wordmark

Betula is the birch, the pioneer tree of Lusatia's post-mining land, and the letters B, T and U stand in its name. The mark is birch bark that also reads as the rows of a list. Running text says “Betula”; only the wordmark stresses B-T-U. The subtitle always says “inoffiziell”.

How the wordmark is spaced

Measured on Inter 800 (em): the stem of the T starts .252 from its origin, its bar ends .587 above the baseline, capitals are .728 high. Small capitals at .72 em reach .524, which leaves .063 of air under the bar; the E is pulled right until the same .063 lies between it and the stem. B–E, T–U and U–L are closed to about .07. Under 28 px the small cut takes over: small capitals at .68 em in weight 500, and .09 of air, so the E and the T do not clot at one pixel per gap.

Every run keeps its own element and there is no white space between them: the margins hang on the elements.

The mark on its grids

A light square (corner radius 22 %) with four dark bars that enter from the edges, left and right in turn, in lengths of about 42, 29, 17 and 46 % of the width. Same rhythm at every size; small sizes get relatively heavier bars.

The icon of the installed app

What a home screen shows is not the mark but a birch leaf that carries it: white, on the green of the birch leaf, with the mark's four bars on it as the marks of bark, entering from its edges left and right in turn as they enter the square. The mark's bars run out to the edges of its square, and a launcher that cuts a circle out of it leaves a white disc with stubs (owner, 2026-09-26); the leaf stands whole in the middle of any shape, and in one colour for Android's themed icons. Google Search shows the leaf too, beside the site's results (owner, 2026-10-01). The site keeps the mark: the tab, the rail, the link previews.

Drawn by app-icon.mjs on the grid of 96 (app-icon.svg). The leaf is three cubic curves on its right, mirrored to the left: the tip at 48 / 14.7, widest at 67.6, the base at 68.2, a broad rounded wedge; the stem curves from 48 / 65.9 to 45.5 / 78.2, 3.6 wide. The bars are 4.7 high, 4.2 apart (tops at 31.3, 40.2, 49.1, 58) and end at 48.9, 50.4, 38.8 and 42.7 inside the leaf: long, middle, short, longest, the rhythm of the mark. They are cut to the leaf by computation, not by a clip path. Colours: the green from #6dac78 at the top (the owner's tone, oklch .6867 .0996 149) to #2d6945 at the bottom (oklch .47 .085 155), a vertical gradient that keeps the PNGs small; the leaf white, its marks #10151f.
