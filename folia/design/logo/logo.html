Betula: icon and wordmark

Betula is the birch, the pioneer tree of Lusatia's post-mining land, and the letters B, T and U stand in its name. The icon is a birch leaf in paper on a square of ink, with four bars of birch bark cut out of it that also read as the rows of a list (owner, 2026-10-15: „Tinte“, „mit Abstand das beste“). It is the same everywhere: the tab, the rail and the start page, the login page of closed testing, the link previews, and the home screen and the splash screen of the installed app. Running text says “Betula”; only the wordmark stresses B-T-U. Beside the wordmark the subtitle says “inoffiziell”; the installed app is called “Betula · Modulkatalog” (owner, 2026-10-15: „Das sieht classy aus“).

How the wordmark is spaced

Measured on Inter 800 (em): the stem of the T starts .252 from its origin, its bar ends .587 above the baseline, capitals are .728 high. Small capitals at .72 em reach .524, which leaves .063 of air under the bar; the E is pulled right until the same .063 lies between it and the stem. B–E, T–U and U–L are closed to about .07. Under 28 px the small cut takes over: small capitals at .68 em in weight 500, and .09 of air, so the E and the T do not clot at one pixel per gap.

The icon on its grid

Drawn by app-icon.mjs on the grid of 96 (app-icon.svg). The leaf is three cubic curves on its right, mirrored to the left: the tip at 48 / 14.7, widest at 67.6, the base at 68.2, a broad rounded wedge. It has no stem and is moved down by 6.5, so that it stands in the middle of the square. The bars are 4.7 high, 4.2 apart (tops at 31.3, 40.2, 49.1, 58), enter from the left and the right in turn and end at 48.9, 50.4, 38.8 and 42.7: long, middle, short, longest. They are cut out of the leaf by computation, not by a clip path or a mask, so the leaf is one outline, the one the page and the server draw (folia_design::ui::LEAF; render-icons.mjs prints it). The square's corner is 21 of 96. Colours: ink #10151f, the text's, and paper #f4f5f7, a white a little softer than the panels; in the dark the square #e9edf5 (the dark theme's --bark) and the leaf in ink. No accent colour, none of the university's colours, and never its lowercase “b-tu” mark.

The leaf stands whole in the middle of any shape a launcher cuts, and in one colour for Android's themed icons (owner, 2026-09-26, of a mark whose bars ran out to the square's edges: a circle left a disc with stubs). Until 2026-10-15 the site had a mark of its own, a white square with four ink bars, and the app's icon was this leaf, white with a stem and black bars on a green gradient (owner: the colours clashed, the gradient looked retro).
